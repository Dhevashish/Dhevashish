01 · THE STARTING POINT

HOW I TEND TO LEARN

Give me the
“why” first.

“I usually don't feel like I understand something until I can explain the idea to myself without the textbook.”

I naturally look for the picture behind a concept. What problem was it trying to solve? What changes if I remove one assumption? What would this look like in a real system?

Once that mental model clicks, the formal stuff becomes much easier to remember. Equations stop feeling like symbols, and code stops feeling like syntax.
